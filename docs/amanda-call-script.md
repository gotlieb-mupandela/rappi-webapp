# Call script: Amanda Sebastiao x Rappi Sports Hub

**Who's on the call:** You (Rappi Sports Hub) and Amanda Sebastiao.
**About Amanda:** Certified lash artist with her own studio in Windhoek, co-owner of Mi.Co fashion, and a following of women who trust her style.
**Based on:** "Partnership Proposal, November 2026", prepared for Amanda.
**Length:** 15–20 minutes. The proposal calls it "a quick call", so keep it light.

**What you want from the call:**
1. Amanda agrees to the partnership.
2. You get her sizes and the looks she likes.
3. You agree how many outfits and how much content, plus a rough date for the first post.

**Have ready before you dial:**
- The proposal PDF open on the outfits pages (pages 5–6)
- A size chart for the women's range (tops, bottoms, tights, trainers)
- Stock levels for the pieces in the eight looks, so you don't promise anything you can't deliver
- Your own answer to "how many outfits / how many posts" (see section 5)
- Something to take notes with

---

## 1. Opening (about 2 minutes)

> "Hi Amanda, it's [your name] from Rappi Sports Hub. Thanks for taking the time, I know studio days are busy. Is now still a good time?"

> "Did you get a chance to look through the proposal we sent?"

- **If yes:** "Great. What stood out to you?" *(Let her talk. Whatever she brings up first is what she cares about most.)*
- **If not really:** "No problem, I'll give you the short version. It'll take two minutes."

> "Here's my plan for the call: I'll go through the idea quickly, ask you a few questions about what would suit you, and then we can agree on next steps. Sound good?"

---

## 2. The proposal in brief (3–4 minutes)

Say this conversationally. Don't read it out.

**Why her:**
> "We're building Rappi Sports Hub's name with women in Namibia who care how they look when they move, work and go out. You already talk to those women every day. Same audience, two brands."

**The deal in one line:**
> "We dress you in Joma, and you show your world wearing it."

**What she gets:**
- Outfits from the women's range, **hers to keep**
- **A first look** at new pieces when they arrive
- Her content **shared on Rappi Sports Hub's pages**, so her brand gets seen by our audience too

**What we'd ask for:**
- Content of her wearing Joma at the studio and when she's out
- **Rappi Sports Hub tagged** so her community can find us
- **Honest feedback** on what her followers like

**Make this clear:**
> "There are no sales targets and no scripts. Your content stays in your own voice. You know your audience better than we do."

**The idea that leads the proposal: "A lash day, in Joma":**
> "We pictured a reel at your studio. You're in the Breath long sleeve and sweatpants with the Neon Lady trainers, doing a full set. Calm, clean, comfortable. Your work and your look in one video."

The three shoot ideas from the proposal, if she wants options:
1. **The lash day reel**: her at work, close-ups of the set, wide shots of her and the studio
2. **Get ready with me**: choosing the outfit, lacing up, heading to the studio
3. **Studio to street**: the same pieces styled for clients in the day and for going out after

> "These are only starting points. If you have a better idea, we'll go with yours."

---

## 3. Questions to ask (5–7 minutes)

Pick the ones that fit the conversation. Starred (★) questions are the ones you need answered before you hang up.

**About her and her audience**
- "Who follows you mostly? Clients, Mi.Co customers, friends?"
- "Which platform do you post on most: Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp status? What works best for you?"
- "What kind of posts get the best response, reels, photos or stories?"
- "Do you work with other brands at the moment? Is there anything we should avoid clashing with?"

**About the looks**
- ★ "Which of the eight looks did you like most?" *(Lash Day, All Black, Gym Session, Off Duty, Game Day, Court Set, Rain or Shine, Layer Up)*
- "Is there anything in there that isn't really you?"
- "Do you train, or is it more lifestyle and comfort for you?" *(Tells you whether to lean on Gym Session/Court Set or on Off Duty/Layer Up)*
- ★ "What are your sizes? Tops, bottoms, leggings and shoe size?"

**About the content**
- "Which of the three shoot ideas feels most natural to you?"
- ★ "How much content feels comfortable for you to start with?"
- ★ "When could you realistically shoot the first one?"
- "Are you happy for us to repost your content on our pages?" *(The proposal promises this. Get a clear yes.)*

**About Mi.Co** *(optional; only if it comes up naturally)*
- "Would you ever want to style Joma with Mi.Co pieces?" *(Don't pitch anything here. Just note her answer for later.)*

---

## 4. Likely objections and how to answer

**"I'm not really a sporty person."**
> "That's exactly why we came to you. Most of the looks aren't about the gym. They're what you'd wear to the studio, on a coffee run or out in the evening. Lash Day, Off Duty and Layer Up are all comfort and style. Pick the ones that feel like you."

**"I don't have much time to make content."**
> "We'd rather have one post that feels like you than five that feel forced. The lash day reel fits around your normal workday, since you're filming what you already do. We'll agree an amount that's easy for you."

**"What's in it for me, apart from free clothes?"**
> "The outfits are yours to keep, and you get a first look at new pieces. We also share your content on our pages, which puts you and your studio in front of our audience. If you'd like a mention of the studio or Mi.Co when we share, we can talk about that."

*(Only offer things you're willing to do. Paid fees, discount codes and commission are **not** in the proposal. If she asks for any of them, see the next objection.)*

**"Do you pay for posts?" / "My rate is…"**
> "The proposal is product-based for now: Joma on us, yours to keep. I'd like to start there, see how your audience responds, and then talk about what a longer partnership could look like."

*(Decide before the call how far you can go here, whether that's a one-off fee, a discount code for her followers or nothing. Don't make up a number on the spot. "Let me come back to you on that" is fine.)*

**"I don't want it to look like an advert."**
> "Neither do we. No scripts and no sales targets. Your voice, your style. All we ask is that you tag Rappi Sports Hub so people can find us."

**"Are these real Joma products?"**
> "Yes. We're the official Joma supplier in Namibia, so everything in your box is genuine Joma. You can see the range at rappisportshub.com."

**"Can I pick different pieces from the ones in the looks?"**
> "Of course. The eight looks are suggestions, and you can mix them however you like. Have a look on the site, and if something else catches your eye, tell me and I'll check we have it in your size."

**"What if I don't like something once I've worn it?"**
> "Tell us. Honest feedback is part of the deal. It helps us know what women here actually want."

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Of course. Is there anything specific you're unsure about? I'm happy to sort it out now." *(If she still wants time:)* "No pressure. Could I send you a short summary on WhatsApp and check in with you on [day]?"

---

## 5. Close and next steps (2–3 minutes)

**Agree the terms the proposal leaves open.** It says: "How many outfits and how much content is something we agree together on the call."

Fill these in before the call so you have a starting offer:

| Item | Your starting offer | Agreed on the call |
|---|---|---|
| Number of outfits / looks | ______ | ______ |
| Content to start with (e.g. reels / posts / stories) | ______ | ______ |
| Platforms | ______ | ______ |
| First post by | ______ | ______ |
| Reposting on Rappi pages | Yes | ______ |

**Repeat back what you agreed:**
> "Let me make sure I've got this right. You'd like [looks], your sizes are [sizes], and you'll start with [content] around [date]. We'll tag each other and we'll share your posts. Is that right?"

**Lay out the next steps** (the proposal's four steps: we talk → you choose → we deliver → you create):
1. **Today:** "I'll send you a short WhatsApp/email summary of what we agreed."
2. **She chooses:** "If you haven't picked your looks yet, send them to me by [day]."
3. **We deliver:** "We'll pack your Joma box in your sizes and get it to you by [date]." *(Ask her: studio or another address? What time suits her?)*
4. **She creates:** "Once you've posted, tag us and we'll share it straight away."

**Finish:**
> "Thanks, Amanda, this is going to look great. I'm looking forward to seeing the lash day reel. I'll message you today with everything we agreed."

---

## After the call (checklist)

- [ ] Send her the summary the same day: looks, sizes, content, dates, delivery address
- [ ] Check stock for every piece in her chosen looks and sizes
- [ ] Pack and deliver the box by the date you gave her
- [ ] Follow her account and turn on notifications so you can repost quickly
- [ ] Note anything you said "I'll come back to you" on, and come back to her on it
- [ ] Two weeks after her first post, ask for the honest feedback the proposal mentions
